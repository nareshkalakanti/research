{
  "intent": "one line — the real business we want",

- names: 10–20 listed names from your knowledge of this niche, not 3 celebrities. tickers if sure.
- exclude: false friends (nuclear medicine vs nuclear power; heart valve vs industrial valve; financing the theme vs operating it).

- Output valid JSON only. No markdown.
