RULES:
1. Pull every number exactly as printed in tables/charts — do not round.
2. For multi-year P&L/balance sheet tables, extract EVERY line item shown,
   not just headline metrics — this data is structured and complete, so
   completeness matters more than for a transcript.
3. Cross-check: total_assets must equal total_equity_and_liabilities for
   each year in the balance sheet. Flag if they don't reconcile.
4. Chairman/MD quotes: paraphrase per copyright rules, but preserve any
   specific % or numeric claims exactly (these are factual data points,
   not the literary expression being protected).
5. "watch_points" and "sentiment_assessment" are the ONE section where
   you are allowed to move beyond pure extraction into analysis —
   specifically, compute derived ratios (e.g., raw material % of
   revenue, receivables/inventory growth vs. revenue growth, operating
   cash flow vs. net profit) to flag quality-of-earnings issues the
   document's own metrics don't call out. Label this section clearly
   as analyst-derived, not company-disclosed.
6. Do not include any data not present in the document — no external
   company facts, no analyst estimates, no comparison to peers unless
   the document itself shows them.
7. Output valid JSON only. No markdown, no preamble, no commentary.

Notes on dynamic keys:
- Prefer q1_fy27_financials (or matching quarter/FY from the document)
  as the quarterly financials key.
- Prefer q1_fy27_domestic_vs_export / q1_fy27_export_country_distribution
  when the deck is for that quarter.
- Prefer shareholding_pattern_as_of_YYYY_MM_DD when a date is shown.
